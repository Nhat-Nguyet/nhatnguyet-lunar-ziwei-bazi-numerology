# School options: where the books disagree

Zi Wei Dou Shu (Tử Vi) and BaZi (Bát Tự) are not one method. Classical texts,
Vietnamese manuals and modern software place some stars differently, start the
day at a different hour, or treat a leap month differently. Most libraries pick
one rule silently. Nhat Nguyet picks a default, says which book it follows, and
lets you switch to the other rules that a named source supports.

Every option below is cited in the reference engine
(the Go engine behind nhatnguyet.org; its file and function are noted in each row). A
variant that no named source supports is not offered: see
[Not offered](#not-offered-and-why).

**Defaults never change.** Without options (or with every option set to its
default) every result is byte-identical to engine 2.1.0, and the shared test
vectors `ca-kiem/tu-vi.json` and `ca-kiem/bat-tu.json` did not change. Options
were added in engine **2.2.0**; their own test vectors are
`ca-kiem/tu-vi-phai.json` (352 cases) and `ca-kiem/bat-tu-phai.json` (80
cases), generated by the Go engine and passed by the TypeScript and Python
ports.

## Zi Wei Dou Shu

| Option | Values (default first) | What changes | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| `fireBell` | `vietnamese`, `quanshu`, `swap-for-female`, `swap-for-yin-male-yang-female` | Hỏa Tinh (Fire Star, 火星) and Linh Tinh (Bell Star, 铃星). All four start from the same palaces by the three-harmony group of the year branch; they differ in direction and in which star takes which start. | see below |
| `leapMonth` | `whole`, `split-at-15`, `next-month` | The lunar month used to place stars for a birth in a leap month: Life and Body palaces (hence the bureau and the 14 major stars), Tả Phù, Hữu Bật, Thiên Hình, Thiên Diêu, Thiên Y, Thiên Giải, Địa Giải, Tam Thai, Bát Tọa, Đẩu Quân, and the monthly periods. Charts not born in a leap month never change. | see below |
| `transformations` | `vietnamese`, `quanshu`, `zhongzhou` | The Four Transformations of the Geng (Canh) stem, for the natal chart, the yearly transformations of a Geng year and the flying transformations from a Geng palace. Other stems never change. | see below |
| `heavenlyKitchen` | `vietnamese`, `chinese` | Thiên Trù (Celestial Kitchen, 天厨) for a Gui (Quý) year only: Xu (Tuất) or Hai (Hợi). | see below |
| `injuryMessenger` | `standard`, `zhongzhou` | Thiên Thương (天伤) and Thiên Sứ (天使) swap places for yin-year men and yang-year women. | see below |

### `fireBell`: Fire and Bell stars

Starting palaces (counted as the Tý hour), by year branch: Dần Ngọ Tuất start
at Sửu and Mão; Thân Tý Thìn at Dần and Tuất; Tỵ Dậu Sửu at Mão and Tuất; Hợi
Mão Mùi at Dậu and Tuất (Fire first, Bell second).

| Value | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `vietnamese` (default) | Yang-year men and yin-year women count Fire forward and Bell backward to the birth hour; yin-year men and yang-year women the other way round. | Tử Vi Đẩu Số Tân Biên (Vân Đằng Thái Thứ Lang), chapter An sao, §8.6.3 |
| `quanshu` | Both stars count forward, whatever the sex and year polarity. | Zi Wei Dou Shu Quan Shu (紫微斗数全书); also iztro |
| `swap-for-female` | Both count forward; for a woman the two starting palaces are swapped. | Việt Viêm Tử, *Tử Vi áo bí biện chứng học*, p. 93 ("Nam tiên khởi Hỏa, Nữ tiên Linh") |
| `swap-for-yin-male-yang-female` | Both count forward; for yin-year men and yang-year women the two starting palaces are swapped. | Quan Xuân Thịnh, *Số Tử Vi Kinh Nghiệm*, vol. 2, pp. 57 to 60: the three printed charts (all yin-year men) are placed this way (Kỷ Sửu, Dậu hour: Fire Mùi, Bell Tý; Ất Dậu, Tý hour: Fire Tuất, Bell Mão; Kỷ Dậu, Thân hour: Fire Ngọ, Bell Hợi). Việt Viêm Tử's worked example, a yang-year woman, fits this reading too. |

The Hợi Mão Mùi start at Dậu follows Việt Viêm Tử (p. 93), Thiên Lương (*Tử Vi
nghiệm lý toàn thư*, p. 171) and the Quan Shu (亥卯未人酉戌房); the digitised
Tân Biên prints Dần there, read as a transcription error.

Why the default: the chart follows Tân Biên for every other star ring, and its
set of stars is the one printed in Vietnamese books. The two `vietnamese` and
`quanshu` rules agree for a Tý-hour birth; otherwise Bell differs on every
yang-male or yin-female chart and Fire on every yin-male or yang-female chart.

Engine: `ViTriHoaLinh`, `ViTriHoaLinhTheoPhai` (`tuvi_saophu_them.go`).

### `leapMonth`: birth in a leap month

| Value | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `whole` (default) | The whole leap month counts as the month it repeats (born in leap month 4: placed as month 4). | Nhat Nguyet's long-standing rule, kept so existing charts do not change |
| `split-at-15` | Days 1 to 15 as the month it repeats, day 16 onward as the next month. | Đẩu Số Tinh Thành, p. 20; iztro's `fixLeap` (on by default in iztro) |
| `next-month` | The whole leap month counts as the next month. | Zi Wei Dou Shu Quan Shu, book 2, 安身命例: 「又若閏月正月生者要在二月內起安身命，凡有閏月具要依此為例」 |

The month after month 12 is month 1; the year stem and branch never change.
The raw chart keeps the true lunar date in `ngaySinhAm` and adds
`placementMonth` when the placement month differs.

Engine: `thangAnTheoPhai` (`tuy_chon_phai.go`).

### `transformations`: the Geng row of the Four Transformations

| Value | Geng (Canh): Lu, Quan, Ke, Ji | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `vietnamese` (default) | Thái Dương, Vũ Khúc, **Thiên Đồng**, **Thái Âm** | Huyền Vi, *Muốn luận đoán đúng số Tử Vi*, p. 4; Thiên Lương, *Tử Vi nghiệm lý toàn thư*, p. 161; Việt Viêm Tử, *Tử Vi áo bí biện chứng học*, p. 86. Wang Tingzhi calls this the version handed down (世传). |
| `quanshu` | Thái Dương, Vũ Khúc, **Thái Âm**, **Thiên Đồng** | Tân Biên, p. 14, table 8.23; Zi Wei Dou Shu Quan Shu (「庚日武阴同为首」); Đẩu Số Tinh Thành vol. 1; iztro |
| `zhongzhou` | Thái Dương, Vũ Khúc, **Thiên Phủ**, **Thiên Đồng** | Wang Tingzhi (王亭之), Zhongzhou school |

Why the default: it is the row of the three Vietnamese manuals most readers own.

Engine: `tuHoaTheoCan` (`tuvi_data.go`), `tuHoaCuaCanTheoPhai` (`tuy_chon_phai.go`).

### `injuryMessenger`: Celestial Injury and Messenger

| Value | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `standard` (default) | Thiên Thương in the Friends palace (Nô Bộc), Thiên Sứ in Health (Tật Ách), for every chart. | Tân Biên §8.21; Zi Wei Dou Shu Quan Shu |
| `zhongzhou` | Swapped for yin-year men and yang-year women. | Zhongzhou school (Wang Tingzhi); also iztro's `algorithm: "zhongzhou"` |

### Birth in the Tý hour (Zi Wei)

The Zi Wei chart takes the lunar date of exactly the Gregorian date entered,
even for a birth between 23:00 and midnight (engine `gio_ty.go`). Some books
count a birth from 23:00 as the next day: to follow them, enter the next date.
The chart says so in its notes when the birth hour is Tý. In the iztro
compatibility layer, `timeIndex` 12 (late Zi) counts as the next day, as in
iztro.

## BaZi

| Option | Values (default first) | What changes | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ziHour` | `next-day-from-23`, `split-at-midnight` | The day pillar of a birth between 23:00 and 24:00, and everything read from it (Day Master, ten gods, stages, symbolic stars, luck pillars' ten gods). | see below |

| Value | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `next-day-from-23` (default) | The day turns at 23:00, the start of the Tý hour: a birth at 23:30 takes the next day's pillar. | Most Zi Ping (Tử Bình) texts (engine `battu.go`, convention 3) |
| `split-at-midnight` | Early and late Tý (tảo Tý, vãn Tý, 早子时/晚子时): the day turns at midnight, so a birth at 23:30 keeps the day pillar of the date entered; the hour pillar is still the Tý hour of the next day (its stem from the next day's stem). | The early/late Zi debate the engine documents; the same rule as lunar-javascript's `EightChar` default (sect 2) |

Example, 2024-01-01 23:30: default day Ất Sửu (Yi Chou), hour Bính Tý (Bing
Zi); `split-at-midnight` day Giáp Tý (Jia Zi), hour Bính Tý. Births outside
23:00 to 24:00 never change.

## Calendar: a school-like option

The Vietnamese lunar calendar is computed for Hanoi time (UTC+7); the Chinese
one for Beijing time (UTC+8). Same rules, different results on 3.71% of days
from 1900 to 2100 (see `doi-chieu/README.md`).

| Option | Values | Where |
|---|---|---|
| `country` | `VN` (default, UTC+7); `CN`, `TW`, `HK`, `MO`, `SG`, `MY`, `ID`, `PH` (UTC+8); `KR`, `JP` (UTC+9) | `toLunar`, `toSolar`, `solarTerms` (`@nhatnguyet/am-lich`, `nhatnguyet.en`), CLI `--country`, MCP `country`, the iztro compatibility layer |
| `timeZone` | hours, overrides `country` | same |

`castZiWei` and `castBaZi` always use the Vietnamese calendar; the iztro
layer takes `country` like the calendar functions.

## How to pass them

TypeScript:

```ts
import { castZiWei } from "@nhatnguyet/tu-vi";
import { castBaZi } from "@nhatnguyet/bat-tu";

castZiWei({ date: "1990-09-19", time: "20:00", gender: "female",
  schools: { fireBell: "quanshu", leapMonth: "split-at-15", transformations: "zhongzhou" } });
castBaZi({ date: "2024-01-01", time: "23:30", gender: "male", schools: { ziHour: "split-at-midnight" } });

// Raw (Vietnamese-keyed) layer: last argument.
lapLaSoTuVi("", "Nữ", 19, 9, 1990, 10, { fireBell: "quanshu" });
lapLaSoBatTu(1, 1, 2024, 23, 30, "Nam", { ziHour: "split-at-midnight" });
```

Python (keys in snake_case or camelCase):

```python
from nhatnguyet import cast_zi_wei, cast_bazi
cast_zi_wei(date="1990-09-19", time="20:00", gender="female",
            schools={"fire_bell": "quanshu", "leap_month": "split-at-15"})
cast_bazi(date="2024-01-01", time="23:30", gender="male", schools={"zi_hour": "split-at-midnight"})
```

Command line (`nhatnguyet` and `python -m nhatnguyet`):

```bash
nhatnguyet ziwei 1990-09-19 20:00 female --fire-bell quanshu --leap-month split-at-15
nhatnguyet bazi 2024-01-01 23:30 male --zi-hour split-at-midnight
```

MCP server: `cast_ziwei` and `render_chart_svg` take `fire_bell`,
`leap_month`, `transformations`, `heavenly_kitchen`, `injury_messenger`;
`cast_bazi` and `render_chart_svg` take `zi_hour`.

iztro compatibility layer (`@nhatnguyet/tu-vi/iztro`): iztro's `fixLeap`
argument is `leapMonth` (`true` is `split-at-15`, `false` is `whole`; an
explicit `schools.leapMonth` wins). To follow iztro's rules:

```ts
import { astro } from "@nhatnguyet/tu-vi/iztro";
astro.bySolar("1990-9-19", 10, "女", true, "en-US",
  { country: "CN", schools: { fireBell: "quanshu", transformations: "quanshu", heavenlyKitchen: "chinese" } });
```

## What the result says

- The chart carries `schools`: only the options that differ from the default
  (absent for a default chart). The raw chart carries the same object, plus
  `placementMonth` when a leap-month rule moved the month.
- `notes` (and so `chartToText`) gain one line, e.g. "School options (not the
  default): Fire and Bell stars: both forward (Quan Shu); Leap month: split at
  day 15."
- The Zi Wei SVG adds a "Schools" row in the centre panel; the BaZi SVG adds
  "day turns at midnight" next to the solar term.
- The raw chart's `caveat` names the rule used and the rule it replaced, in
  three languages.
- An unknown option or value throws (`RangeError` in TypeScript, `ValueError`
  in Python), listing the accepted values.

## Parity with iztro

`doi-chieu/iztro-tuong-thich.test.mjs` compares the iztro compatibility layer
with iztro 2.6.5 on 610 charts (37,210 star positions, Beijing time, iztro's
`fixLeap` both on and off, so leap-month births included). Differences that
remain are counted, not errors:

| | Default | With `{ fireBell: "quanshu", transformations: "quanshu", heavenlyKitchen: "chinese" }` |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fire and Bell star positions | 484 | **0** |
| Geng-year transformations | 126 | **0** |
| Gui-year Celestial Kitchen | 59 | **0** |
| Leap-month placement (`fixLeap` mapped to `leapMonth`) | 0 | 0 |
| Major-star brightness (five-level Vietnamese scale vs iztro's seven) | 5,638 | 5,638 |

## Not offered, and why

These disagreements are documented in the engine but are not options yet:

- **Tân (Xin) stem transformations.** Only the digitised Tân Biên gives Thiên
  Lương as Hóa Quyền, and it has not been checked against a printed copy; every
  other book in the library gives Thái Dương.
- **Nhâm (Ren) stem, Thiên Phủ as Hóa Khoa.** Attributed to "some schools"; no
  named source in the library.
- **Thiên Khôi, Thiên Việt for Canh**: Tân Biên Ngọ and Dần (default); the Quan
  Shu and Đẩu Số Tinh Thành group Canh with Giáp and Mậu (Sửu and Mùi).
- **Direction of the Tràng Sinh ring** (Tân Biên by year polarity and sex; the
  Quan Shu by sex alone), **first ten-year period** (Tân Biên from the Life
  palace; the Quan Shu from the next palace), **monthly periods** (from the
  yearly Grand Duke palace as the Quan Shu; Tân Biên from the minor-limit
  palace), **Thiên Quý**, **brightness scale**.
- **BaZi**: hidden stems of Thìn, Tuất, Sửu, Mùi and Ngọ (Ziping Zhenquan vs
  Sanming Tonghui), twelve stages of yin stems, life palace by sectional term
  or by middle term, the two conception-pillar methods.

Each is a candidate for a future option once its sources are entered the same
way. The engine's `khacNhau` blocks (`httpapi/tri_thuc_bang.go`) and the
chart's `caveat` and `quyUoc` lines already state them.
