# Nhat Nguyet API reference

## Modules

| Module | Description |
| ------ | ------ |
| [@nhatnguyet/am-lich](@nhatnguyet/am-lich.md) | Vietnamese, Chinese, Korean and Japanese lunisolar calendars: Gregorian to lunar and back, the sexagenary cycle (can chi), the 24 solar terms to the minute, hour branches, auspicious hours. |
| [@nhatnguyet/bat-tu](@nhatnguyet/bat-tu.md) | BaZi (Four Pillars, Bát Tự): pillars by solar term to the minute, hidden stems, Ten Gods, symbolic stars, luck pillars, SVG and text output. |
| [@nhatnguyet/cli](@nhatnguyet/cli.md) | The text renderers behind the `nhatnguyet` command line: Zi Wei grid, BaZi, lunar date, solar terms, numerology and festival tables, hexagrams, Six Lines charts, life gua, flying star charts and Luban rulers, with terminal width handling for CJK text. Command reference: the [package README](https://github.com/Nhat-Nguyet/nhatnguyet-lunar-ziwei-bazi-numerology/tree/main/js/packages/cli#readme). |
| [@nhatnguyet/fengshui](@nhatnguyet/fengshui.md) | Feng shui rules of traditional schools: Eight Mansions life gua (Kua number) and house gua, the 24 mountains, Xuan Kong flying star charts for periods 1 to 9, annual stars, the three Luban rulers, and SVG drawings of the compass and of a flying star chart. These are the rules a school states, not verified effects. |
| [@nhatnguyet/iching](@nhatnguyet/iching.md) | The I Ching (Yijing, Book of Changes): the 64 hexagrams with names in English, pinyin, Chinese and Vietnamese, the eight trigrams, the Eight Palaces, nuclear, opposite, reversed and changed hexagrams, three-coin and Plum Blossom casting, the Six Lines (najia) chart, the daily hexagram of the Gua Qi calendar, the public domain Zhou Yi text with James Legge's 1882 translation, and an SVG drawing. |
| [@nhatnguyet/le-hoi](@nhatnguyet/le-hoi.md) | Vietnamese festivals and holidays with their Gregorian dates for any year from 1900 to 2100 (data under CC BY 4.0). |
| [@nhatnguyet/than-so-hoc](@nhatnguyet/than-so-hoc.md) | Pythagorean numerology from a full name and a birth date: life path, expression, soul urge, personality, personal year and more, SVG and text output. |
| [@nhatnguyet/tu-vi](@nhatnguyet/tu-vi.md) | Zi Wei Dou Shu (Purple Star Astrology, Tử Vi): the twelve palaces and their stars, ten-year and yearly periods, yearly stars, chart patterns, SVG and text output. |
